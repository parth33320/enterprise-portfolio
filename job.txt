Collins Aerospace is seeking a Procurement Professional to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for managing the Procure-to-Pay process for indirect materials, ensuring timely and efficient purchasing activities that support operational requirements and Supply Chain objectives.

The ideal candidate will collaborate with internal stakeholders and suppliers to drive cost-effective procurement solutions, maintain compliance with company policies, and contribute to continuous process improvements. This position offers the opportunity to play a key role in supporting business operations while helping drive innovation and excellence in aerospace procurement.

What You Will Do:
Identify qualified suppliers for requirements, fully leveraging existing agreements;

Manage purchase order placement process from requisition to invoice payment for assigned commodities within the Indirect team;

Obtain price quotations and negotiate pricing and delivery in support of commodity supplier and cost strategies;

Analyze supplier quotes and makes sourcing recommendations sourcing decisions

Ensure product on-time delivery to schedule

Initiate supplier negotiations on price, lead time and terms & conditions

Complete purchase order documentation in accordance with company policies and procedures

Ensure timely placement of orders to support business needs and meet team defined turn times;

Strong communication skills to interface with requisitioner and other functional groups

Ability to work as a team member independently based on additional projects as assigned to support Supply Chain organization.

Typically requires a University Degree in Business Administration, International Business, Industrial Engineering, Logistics or a related field with a minimum of 2 years of relevant experience.

Advanced proficiency in English

Strong communication skills, with the ability to communicate fluently and professionally in written and spoken English.

Proficiency in SAP and Ariba (Procurement functions, Business Warehouse, Business Objects).

Advanced skills in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.

Familiarity with lean principles and process improvement strategies.

Knowledge of Indirect commodities in a manufacturing or aerospace environment.

Experience in NPI (New Product Introduction) purchasing, including sourcing and procurement.
