Standard Job Description
Candidate will provide analytical support for group responsible for Lockheed Martin’s Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) activities. Responsibilities will include but not be limited to: 

- Provide detailed financial models on prospective Mergers and Acquisition (M&A) targets

- Conduct research and analysis in support of the initial screening and qualification of M&A candidates

- Support M&A transaction as needed including assisting with due diligence activity

- Develop reports, analysis, and presentations on both the Corporation's M&A activity as well as on M&A activity within the Aerospace / Defense industry


Basic Qualifications
Strong familiarity with financial concepts (including accounting and valuation) obtained either through education or work experience. 

Proficiency in key Microsoft Office applications including Powerpoint, Excel, and Word

Experience using spreadsheets to perform financial analysis

Presentation skillset to senior internal and external stakeholders. Ability to coordinate across businesses and functions.

Desired Skills
2-4 years of related work experience

MBA level or other advanced financial coursework helpful 

Familiarity with financial information systems

Defense/Aerospace industry experience

Organized and attentive to detail

Effective verbal and written communication skills
